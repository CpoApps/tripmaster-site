Privacy policy.

Effective date: 9 September 2026
Publisher: Sipke Lautenbach
Contact: cpoapps@gmail.com

TripMaster is a rally instrument and personal event notebook. It does not contain advertising, analytics SDKs, or a developer-operated backend, and it does not require a TripMaster account.

Location and event data

With your permission, the app uses precise location to measure distance, speed, and direction and to save timestamped GPS tracks. Location recording can continue in the background during an active event or special, with the iOS background location indicator. You can pause recording, stop a special, or revoke permission in iOS Settings. Force-quitting stops location recording.

The app stores event names, trip readings, special timing results, manually entered controls and notes, and GPS tracks on your device. Interrupted sessions reopen paused. Finished events are kept as event documents. The publisher does not receive these files automatically.

Optional iCloud Drive

If you enable iCloud storage in Events, finished event documents, including their GPS tracks and notes, are copied to your private iCloud Drive container. Apple manages their storage and transmission under your Apple Account and its privacy terms. Other devices using the same account can download the event documents. Local copies remain available offline. Turning off sync stops future sync; it does not remove existing copies from iCloud or other devices.

Exports and support

GPX and text exports are shared only when you choose to export them. GPS files reveal where and when an event took place. The destination or recipient you choose handles that copy under its own policies.

If you email support, we receive the information you include and use it to respond. Please avoid sending full GPS tracks unless they are needed for your request.

Retention and removal

Event files remain until removed. In version 1.2 or later, use Delete event in Events to remove a finished event and its track locally. When iCloud sync is enabled, this deletion propagates to other devices running version 1.2 or later. Offline deletions are queued until sync resumes. Small deletion records containing an event identifier and deletion date are retained locally and in iCloud to prevent old copies from returning. Exported files are separate and must be removed separately. Removing the app removes its local storage but does not automatically remove iCloud documents. Update all devices before managing a shared archive.

Changes

Any material changes will be reflected in this policy. Questions: cpoapps@gmail.com.

This support website

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its GitHub Pages documentation and privacy statement. Email links open your chosen email app.
