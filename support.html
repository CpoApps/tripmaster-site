Common questions

How do I start recording?

Open Trip and tap Record. Allow location access when prompted and enable Precise Location in iOS Settings. GPS status shows whether a usable fix is available. Pause stops the main trip; an independently running special must be stopped separately.

How do I run a regularity?

Open Special, configure the target average or distance-based speed-change table, and tap Start special. It starts at zero independently of the main trip. Stop special saves its result. You can also schedule a start using the rally clock or configure a maximum-time bogey special. Set the target table before starting.

Why is GPS weak, stale or unavailable?

Check Location Services and TripMaster’s permission in iOS Settings. Use Precise Location and allow time for a fix with a clear view of the sky. Buildings, tunnels and vehicle mounting can affect reception. Poor or stale fixes are rejected and may create gaps in your GPX track.

Will recording continue when the screen is locked?

Background location recording is configured while a trip or special is active, subject to iOS permissions and conditions. Keep the app running: force-quitting stops recording. Check this behavior on your own device before an event.

How do I export a GPX track?

Record actual GPS positions, then choose Export GPX in Rally log or Setup, or open a finished event in Events and export from its details. Choose a destination in Files. Demo mode contains no GPS track. Tripmeter corrections do not change the original GPS coordinates.

How do I save an event to iCloud?

Choose Finish & archive event in Setup. In Events, enable Store finished events in iCloud. Sign into the same Apple Account on your devices and enable iCloud Drive. The status line shows availability and pending uploads; a local copy remains available offline. Only finished events sync.

How can I remove event data?

In version 1.2, open Events and choose Delete event below the event, then confirm. Deletion removes the local event and syncs to iCloud and other devices running version 1.2 or later when sync is enabled. Offline deletions wait until sync resumes. Exported copies remain separate. Update all devices before managing a shared archive.

How do I correct a control or time window?

Use the three-dot menu beside an item in Rally log, Roadbook or the time-window list to edit or delete it. Editing a log entry does not change your tripmeters or original GPS track.

Can I change language, units or appearance?

Open Setup. Choose English, Dutch or the system language; your region and number formats; metric or imperial units; and System, Dark or Light appearance. These preferences apply immediately.

Can I practice without driving?

Enable Demo mode in Setup before you have an active recording or recorded GPS track. Demo mode is clearly labelled and produces simulated readings without inventing GPS coordinates. Finish the current event first if you need to change modes.

Is it permitted in my rally?

Check your event’s regulations. Some organisers permit distance and timing instruments but restrict navigation, phones or tablets. TripMaster does not replace your official roadbook, control card or certified timing equipment.
